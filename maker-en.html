0If you already have a Nest (otherwise, skip this)

Enter your Nest's address and press "Load." Its current contents go into the fields below, and you can fix them from there.

1About your Nest

These three are always required.

The address you see when you open the Nest in a browser. It must end with / (if you forget, we'll add it).

Up to about 160 characters is best. It appears as-is on the map's card.

2Your feedsoptional

RSS addresses for your blog, note, and so on. If you write them here, a cottage collects your new updates and your circle on the map glows.

For note (a Japanese blogging platform), it's https://note.com/(your ID)/rss.

3Nests you knowoptional

Names and addresses of Nests you'd like to connect to. Write one here, and one arrow reaches out on the map. If the other party writes you back, the arrow becomes two-way.

4Your other placesoptional

Your places outside this Nest: X, a profile page, a book's store page, and so on. They appear in a cottage's news cards and at a Nest's entrance.

5Done

The contents below are your nest.json. Download it, or copy it.
